The 2nd Workshop on Governance and Policy in East Asia

政治学・行政学・公共政策学を中心とする社会科学領域の研究者が集い、 研究交流とネットワーク形成を図るワークショップです。

This workshop brings together researchers in the social sciences, particularly in political science, public administration, and public policy, to promote academic exchange and build scholarly networks.
